This tab:
Capture from the screen - works with any viewer. The extension scrolls through the PDF in that tab and cuts each page out of the screenshots as its own image (or makes one long image if the pages cannot be told apart). It is as sharp as the screen: zoom the viewer in first for sharper text. Keep the tab visible and do not scroll while it works.
Or catch the PDF file itself while it opens - press Start, then close the PDF in that tab and open it again. Chrome shows a "started debugging this browser" bar while it watches; the website cannot see it.
